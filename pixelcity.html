PixelCity

Build a region out of big pixels and keep it moving. Every resident owns a car. They drive to work in the morning, shop and visit parks in the afternoon, and head home to bed at night. The little faces over homes show how residents feel about their commute.

  • Roads: drag to draw streets (with sidewalks), country roads (none), highways or one-ways. Lay highways side by side to make them as wide as you like: half the rows run each way. Roads across water become bridges. Add traffic lights and overpasses at busy crossings.
  • Buildings need a road next to them. Click to place, or drag to paint rows of houses. Docks go on a shore, half over the water.
  • Parking: offices and shops have fewer spaces than visitors. Full lots send drivers circling to a Parking Lot or Garage, or home unhappy.
  • Walking: sidewalks, footpaths, plazas and parks let people walk short trips. Metro stations take longer trips off the road, but a station is only useful once you draw Metro Rails (Metro tab) between it and another station.
  • Freight: farms, quarries, fisheries and rigs load their goods onto trucks at the kerb. Draw surface Freight Track (Transit tab, I) between Train Yards; each train carries 12-18 cars in runs of three, mixing passengers, materials, liquids and gas. Trains plan multi-stop tours between up to three yards, and a Freight Line tool gives chosen yards a dedicated loop. Track is split into signal blocks; a block turns red while a train holds the leg, and no two trains can occupy the same block. Rail Signal (E) adds a block boundary by hand. Cargo Ports send ships to other ports and islands, and Distribution Terminals sort truck cargo onto trains and quays. Oil and gas buildings must sit within 5 blocks of a train yard.
  • Region: nine connected districts, all simulated at once. Zoom out past a whole district to see them all, then click one to build there. Cars, boats and planes travel across all of them.
  • Turn on Congestion to see jams as a green-to-red gradient. Underground strips the surface away and shows the metro network: rails, junction blocks where lines meet, reversing blocks where a train can turn back, and the trains themselves. Save & share in the menu makes a save file or a share code.
